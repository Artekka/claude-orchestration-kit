# Decisions — what the human decided, and when

> The human's rulings that the code must follow and the repo cannot show by itself: product choices,
> numbers, scope cuts, "do it this way". The seat records each one as it is made, in the human's
> words where possible. Newest at the bottom. Never edit a decision's text after the fact: change
> one by adding a new entry that **supersedes** it, and mark the old one `Superseded by D-0nn`.
> `/orchestration-kit:release-review` reads this file; between releases the seat's decision check
> (orchestrate skill, §2) re-asks the human about entries that have gone stale.
